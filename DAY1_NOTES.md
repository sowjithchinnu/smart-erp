# SmartERP - Day 1 Notes

## MVP Features

### Ledgers
- Customer Ledger
- Supplier Ledger
- Item/Stock Ledger

### Vouchers
- Sales Voucher
- Purchase Voucher

## Business Rules

1. A user can create multiple companies.
2. A company can have multiple customers.
3. A company can have multiple suppliers.
4. A company can have multiple stock items.
5. Purchase vouchers increase stock quantity.
6. Sales vouchers decrease stock quantity.
7. Every customer has a ledger.
8. Every supplier has a ledger.
9. Sales vouchers generate invoices automatically.
